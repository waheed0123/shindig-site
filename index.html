Shindig: Party Games Support Shindig is a party game hub for one phone and a group of friends. Pick a game, pass the phone round and play. Contact us Questions, bug reports or ideas: shindigpartyapp@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a few days. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you use, and which game it happened in. Common questions How do I restart a game or go back to the menu? Tap the pause button in any game. You can restart the game or return to the main menu. I bought Premium but still see adverts. Open the Premium screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with. How do I cancel the monthly subscription? On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Shindig Premium and tap Cancel Subscription. Apple manages all billing and refunds, and you can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Do I need an internet connection? The games work offline.
