Privacy Policy

Shindig: Party Games · Last updated 8 November 2026

This policy explains what information Shindig: Party Games (“Shindig”, “the app”) uses. Contact: shindigpartyapp@gmail.com.

What we collect

Shindig has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your email address, contacts, photos or precise location. Game settings and your Premium status are stored on your device only.

Online play

Some games offer an optional online mode where friends join a room with a code. If you use it, the display name you type in, a random identifier generated on your device, and the moves or answers needed to play the game are sent to a Google Firebase Realtime Database so that everyone in the room can see them. This information is not linked to an account or to your identity, is used only to run the game, and rooms are deleted automatically after the game ends or after a few hours. We do not use it for advertising. Please do not enter your real full name or any personal details as your display name. If you never use online play, none of this applies.

Adverts

The free version shows a full-screen advert after a game ends. Adverts are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure adverts, Google and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), and information about how you interact with adverts. If you are in the UK or Europe you will see a consent message, and you can change your choice later from the privacy options in the app.

On iPhone, the app asks permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you tap “Ask App Not to Track” the advertising identifier is not used for personalised adverts, and you can still play everything. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Shindig Premium (a one-off purchase or a monthly subscription) is processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple tells the app whether Premium is active so that adverts can be removed.

Children

Shindig is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data held by Google through adverts, you can use Google's own controls at myadcenter.google.com. If you are in the UK or EU you may also contact us with any request, or complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.
