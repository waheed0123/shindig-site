Privacy Policy Shindig: Party Games · Last updated 4 November 2026 This policy explains what information Shindig: Party Games (“Shindig”, “the app”) uses. Contact: shindigpartyapp@gmail.com. What we collect Shindig has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not run our own servers that store information about you. Game settings and your Premium status are stored on your device only. Adverts The free version shows a full-screen advert after a game ends. Adverts are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure adverts, Google and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), and information about how you interact with adverts. If you are in the UK or Europe you will see a consent message, and you can change your choice later from the privacy options in the app. On iPhone, the app asks permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you tap “Ask App Not to Track” the advertising identifier is not used for personalised adverts, and you can still play everything. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services. Purchases Shindig Premium (a one-off purchase or a monthly subscription) is processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple tells the app whether Premium is active so that adverts can be removed. Children Shindig is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Your rights Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data held by Google through adverts, you can use Google's own controls at myadcenter.google.com. If you are in the UK or EU you may also contact us with any request, or complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).
